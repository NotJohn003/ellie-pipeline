Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Ellie Pipeline is a private automation tool used by a single operator, Ian Steve Hinkson, to produce video episodes for the Easy English Stories YouTube channel. It has no other users. It offers no accounts, no sign-up and no public service.

What the app accesses

When the operator signs in with their own Google Account, the app requests access to Google Drive. It uses that access only to read video, audio and text files from folders in that same account, and to write finished video files back to folders in that same account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access the Google Account of any other person.
  • It does not collect, store or process personal information about visitors to this website.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or to train machine learning models.

Where data is held

Files move directly between the operator's Google Drive and a private server controlled by the operator. Access credentials are stored only on that server and are not shared with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Copies of files fetched for processing are deleted from the server once they are no longer needed. The operator can revoke this app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all access.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
