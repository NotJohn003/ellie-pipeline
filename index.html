Ellie Pipeline

Ellie Pipeline is a private, single-operator automation tool used to produce animated episodes for the Easy English Stories YouTube channel.

It assembles narration audio, alignment data and rendered footage into finished episode masters. It is operated by one person for their own channel and is not offered as a public product or service.

What it connects to

The tool signs in to the operator's own Google Account and uses the Google Drive API to read episode source assets and to write finished masters back to the operator's own Drive folders. It does not access anyone else's data.
